Questions about Chhatpat

What is Chhatpat?

Chhatpat! is a free rooftop parkour game for Android phones by Viidesi Studios. You run, leap, climb and slide across the rooftops of a procedurally built Indian city to finish a filmi errand before the clock runs out.

Is Chhatpat free?

Yes. Chhatpat is free to download on Google Play. There are no pop-up ads: adverts only appear on the billboards inside the city.

Does Chhatpat work offline?

Yes. The whole game plays offline. Play with friends also works without internet, over the same Wi-Fi or a phone hotspot.

How do I play Chhatpat with friends?

One phone hosts a game and gets a 5-digit game ID. Up to 3 friends on the same Wi-Fi or hotspot type the ID in. Everyone’s house stands in one shared city and you all race the same errand. Each player can stake up to 3 things from their home, and the winner takes all.

Is the Chhatpat city the same every time?

No. Every run builds a brand-new Indian city with havelis, chawls, bazaars, temples, masjids, gurudwaras and stepwells. Today’s City is the exception: every day all players get the same city and race for the best time before a new one arrives at midnight.

Which Indian landmarks are in the game?

The Red Fort, Qutub Minar, India Gate, Humayun’s Tomb, Jama Masjid, Akshardham, the Lotus Temple and Agrasen ki Baoli rise over the rooftops.

Is Chhatpat like Subway Surfers or Temple Run?

It shares the quick, one-thumb fun of a runner, but Chhatpat is an open rooftop city rather than a three-lane track. You choose your own route across roofs, gullies and stairwells, climb pipes, ride kites and sky buses, and walk in through front doors.
