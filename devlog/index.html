    Ten heroes, ten desi emergencies

    Kamla Dadi's serial, Pappu's baraat, Mummy's pressure cooker and seven more. Meet the ten heroes of Chhatpat and the filmi emergencies that send them running across the rooftops.

    Play with friends without internet, on one Wi-Fi

    How Chhatpat's local multiplayer works with no data at all. A 5-digit game ID, up to 4 phones on one Wi-Fi or hotspot, a shared city with everyone's house in it, and a pot of home objects.

    How Chhatpat builds a new Indian city every run

    Gullies 2.6 metres wide, avenues built to Indian road standards, 400 hand-painted surfaces and a crowd that walks into shops. How Chhatpat's procedural desi city is put together on a phone.
