The game

Chhatpat in one minute

Chhatpat! is a game by India, for India. Not a postcard India: the one outside your window right now, with chai on the boil, a baraat blocking the road, aunties drying papad on the roof and kites crossing the evening sky.

You play one of ten heroes, each with a filmi emergency and a clock. Kamla Dadi will not miss episode 4,512 of her serial. Pappu's baraat cannot move without his dhol. Mummy's pressure cooker is on its third seeti. To get there in time you run the rooftops: no lanes, no rails, just the whole city and whatever route you can find through it.

Every run builds a brand-new city of havelis, chawls, temples, masjids, gurudwaras and stepwells, painted with over 400 hand-made Indian surfaces from jaali to Ajrakh. Landmarks rise over the roofs: Red Fort, Qutub Minar, India Gate, Humayun's Tomb, Jama Masjid, Akshardham, Lotus Temple, Agrasen ki Baoli. The streets are full of chaiwalas, golgappe wale and dabbawalas, autos below and sky buses above.

Features

Twelve things in the game

01

Rooftop parkour

Sprint from chhat to chhat, leap across gullies, climb the green pipes, catch ledges, slide under washing lines and air-dash over gaps. Pick a lock, walk in through a front door and out the back.

02

Errands against the clock

Run to the kirana, the halwai, the chemist and the darzi before time runs out. Every hero’s story is a timed filmi emergency.

03

Patang, chai, gulel

Ride a kite over the roofs, gulp chai to run faster, fire the gulel to swing to the highest point ahead, and grab 12 kinds of street boosts.

04

Play with friends, offline

Up to 4 players on the same Wi-Fi or a phone hotspot. Host a game, share the 5-digit ID, race the same errand in one shared city. Winner takes the pot.

05

Your own ghar

Your house stands in every city and every run starts on its terrace. Decorate it with 284 things you find out in the city.

06

Today's City

Every day everyone gets the same city and the same race. Set your best time before a new city arrives at midnight. The week’s best become the Shehar ke Sher.

07

Dukaan and outfits

55 outfits, from the Neon Kurta to the Superhero Lungi and Gold Chain Bhai. Every outfit brings its own power. Take a start kit of boosts into a run.

08

Puzzles and badges

Golden puzzle pieces hidden off the route complete picture puzzles and win new stories and outfits. 20 achievements, leaderboards and cloud save with Google Play Games.

09

Difficulty your way

Aaram Se, Josh Mein, Khatarnaak, or Apna Level, which learns how you play.

10

Controls your way

Joystick, gyro, a left-handed layout, buttons you can drag where you like, and full gamepad support with Xbox, PlayStation and Nintendo glyphs.

11

English or Desi

Play in plain English, or in Desi mode: Hinglish full of pop-culture references.

12

Built for every phone

Five graphics modes, from Smooth for 4 GB phones to Ultra for flagships, and 30 to 144 FPS. No pop-up ads: adverts appear only on the city’s own billboards.

FAQ

Chhatpat questions, answered

What is Chhatpat?

Chhatpat! is a free rooftop parkour game for Android phones by Viidesi Studios. You run, leap, climb and slide across the rooftops of a procedurally built Indian city to finish a filmi errand before the clock runs out.

Is Chhatpat free?

Yes. Chhatpat is free to download on Google Play. There are no pop-up ads: adverts only appear on the billboards inside the city.

Does Chhatpat work offline?

Yes. The whole game plays offline. Play with friends also works without internet, over the same Wi-Fi or a phone hotspot.

How do I play Chhatpat with friends?

One phone hosts a game and gets a 5-digit game ID. Up to 3 friends on the same Wi-Fi or hotspot type the ID in. Everyone’s house stands in one shared city and you all race the same errand. Each player can stake up to 3 things from their home, and the winner takes all.

Is the Chhatpat city the same every time?

No. Every run builds a brand-new Indian city with havelis, chawls, bazaars, temples, masjids, gurudwaras and stepwells. Today’s City is the exception: every day all players get the same city and race for the best time before a new one arrives at midnight.

Which Indian landmarks are in the game?

The Red Fort, Qutub Minar, India Gate, Humayun’s Tomb, Jama Masjid, Akshardham, the Lotus Temple and Agrasen ki Baoli rise over the rooftops.

Is Chhatpat like Subway Surfers or Temple Run?

It shares the quick, one-thumb fun of a runner, but Chhatpat is an open rooftop city rather than a three-lane track. You choose your own route across roofs, gullies and stairwells, climb pipes, ride kites and sky buses, and walk in through front doors.

Can I play Chhatpat in Hindi or Hinglish?

You can play in plain English or in Desi mode, which is Hinglish full of pop-culture references.

Which phones can run Chhatpat?

Chhatpat has five graphics modes, from Smooth for 4 GB phones to Ultra for flagships, and runs from 30 to 144 FPS. It needs Android 8.0 or newer.

Does Chhatpat support game controllers?

Yes. Gamepads and Bluetooth controllers work, with button glyphs for Xbox, PlayStation and Nintendo pads, remapping and rumble. On touch you can use a joystick, gyro, a left-handed layout or drag the buttons where you like.

Who makes Chhatpat?

Viidesi Studios, an independent Indian game studio. Chhatpat is its first game, and more games are in the works.

Can brands advertise inside Chhatpat?

Yes. Chhatpat’s city has thousands of in-world billboards, hoardings and LED screens, and weekly brand hunts where players search the city for hidden items. Write to the studio to book a campaign.
