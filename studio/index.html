The India outside the window, in a game

Games set in India often look like a museum or a postcard. We make the real, noisy, loving India of right now: the WhatsApp forward, the power cut before the serial, the cow that will not move.

Our games are built for the phones people in India actually own. Chhatpat runs on 4 GB phones as well as flagships, plays offline, and lets friends play together on one Wi-Fi with no data. Every sign, snack and costume comes from home.

For brands

Advertise inside Chhatpat's city

Chhatpat has no pop-up ads. Brands appear the way they do on a real Indian street: on the hoardings, walls and screens of the city itself, seen by players running past.

FAQ

Questions about Viidesi

What is Viidesi Studios?

Viidesi Studios is an independent video game studio from India. It makes games by India, for India, starting with Chhatpat!, a desi rooftop parkour game for Android.

What games has Viidesi Studios made?

Chhatpat! Desi Rooftop Run is the first, free on Google Play. More games are in development.

How can a brand advertise inside Chhatpat?

Chhatpat’s city carries thousands of in-world hoardings, wall paints, banners and LED screens, plus weekly brand hunts where players search the city for hidden items. Adverts are part of the world, never pop-ups. Email the studio to plan a campaign.

Is Viidesi Studios hiring or open to collaborations?

The studio is small and open to talking with artists, musicians, voice actors and other Indian creators. Email the studio with your work.
